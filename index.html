How big is it?

Milky Way

Roughly 100,000 light-years across, with hundreds of billions of stars. The Sun sits about 26,000 light-years from the centre.

Andromeda

The nearest large spiral galaxy, about 2.5 million light-years away and a little larger than the Milky Way.

Observable universe

About 93 billion light-years across and home to hundreds of billions of galaxies. Its oldest light has travelled for 13.8 billion years.
